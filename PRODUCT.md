# Product

## Register

product

## Users

An individual who wants a fast, dependable place to shorten links, revisit previously created links, and understand basic click activity without account or team-management overhead.

## Product Purpose

Turn long URLs into memorable short links, keep a local history of created links, and make lightweight click analytics immediately useful. Success means a link can be created and copied in seconds, while older links and their activity remain easy to inspect.

## Brand Personality

Technical, modern, and confident. The interface uses bold typography and precise language while staying calm enough for repeated everyday use.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic marketing-page decoration, purple-gradient SaaS styling, glass-heavy dashboards, dense enterprise administration, and playful visuals that distract from the shortening workflow.

## Design Principles

1. Put the shortening action first and make its result immediately copyable.
2. Show useful operational detail without making a personal tool feel like enterprise software.
3. Use bold type and disciplined spacing to communicate technical confidence.
4. Make every state explicit: loading, success, validation errors, empty history, and unavailable services.
5. Keep analytics understandable at a glance and progressively reveal details.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.1 AA for contrast and interaction. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, semantic status messages, color-independent state cues, and reduced-motion preferences.
